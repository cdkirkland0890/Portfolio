A few details help us understand your application.

Please complete each required field for every child, up to 10 children. Do not include diagnoses, treatment details, financial information, or other unrelated sensitive details.

Your privacy matters.
3-7FA Battalion Chaplain is the only person authorized to review applications. Your name, email, battery selection, and each child’s age group, gender, clothing sizes, interests and hobbies, wish list items, and sensory and allergy concerns will be taken into consideration by the Chaplain. Volunteers will be provided the age, clothing sizes, wish list, and hobbies of recipient for gift ideas. No other information will be shared. Sensory and allergy concerns may include sensitive health information. Do not include diagnoses, treatment details, financial information, or other unrelated sensitive details.
